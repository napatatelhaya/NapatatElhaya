Our Journey

15 Years of Excellence & Trust

Since the establishment of "Nabatat Elhaya", we have been more than just an export company; we are a trusted bridge connecting Egyptian farms to global markets. Over 15 years, we have diligently built a robust and flexible supply chain, relying on a wide network of trusted local suppliers.

We understand that every product has its unique nature. Therefore, we designed our supply network to be fast and adaptable, tailored to the specific requirements of each herb, spice, or seed, ensuring our clients receive the product at its peak freshness and quality, regardless of the destination.
